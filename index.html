Ceren Ocak, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Instructional Technology

Department of Leadership, Technology & Human Development · College of Education · Georgia Southern University

My research focuses on computer science education in K–12 settings, with particular attention to how children develop the cognitive processes underlying computational thinking over time.

Ph.D. Learning, Design, and Technology, University of Georgia, 2020 · M.S. Curriculum and Instruction and B.S. Elementary Science Education, Middle East Technical University, Ankara

Google Scholar · ORCID

Research

  • Embodiment of computational thinkingHow young learners develop computational ideas through gesture, discourse, and interaction with tools and the immediate environment.
  • Multimodal interaction analysisDeveloping methodological approaches for capturing and interpreting multimodal data (e.g., speech, gesture, and discourse), including training a machine-learning algorithm to detect patterns in learner activity and validating its performance against human analysis.
  • Computer-supported collaborative learningHow pairs and groups build shared understanding in technology-rich settings, including educational robotics, and how these methodological insights translate into practical guidance for instructional designers and educators applying embodied cognition in classrooms.
